Entropi depth lithography uses Apple Depth Pro 2 S (WASM) and B (WebGPU) from
apple-aiml-research/ml-depth-pro-2, revision
aa389cbb0b74341664ed663a0966c859905cd6c0. The ONNX file is a derivative of the
official checkpoint, exported by scripts/export-depth-pro-2.py with 8-bit
weight-only MatMul quantization, self-hosted and verified against the SHA-256
in models/manifest.json. The weights are licensed exclusively for
non-commercial research under the Apple Machine Learning Research Model
license: models/LICENSE.txt.

ONNX Runtime Web 1.30.0 is MIT licensed. The JavaScript modules (WASM and
WebGPU builds) are vendored; the matching WASM binaries are fetched from the pinned jsDelivr npm distribution
and verified against models/manifest.json. License: vendor/ONNXRUNTIME-LICENSE.txt.

Three.js 0.186.1 and OrbitControls are MIT licensed and bundled from the npm
dependency. Its license is included in node_modules/three/LICENSE at build time.

Depth is estimated, relative, and represents visible surfaces. It is not a metric
scan or a complete reconstruction of unseen surfaces. Camera photos stay local.
